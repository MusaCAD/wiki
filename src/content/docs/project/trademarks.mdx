## The Musa CAD name and logo

The Musa CAD name and logo identify this project. You're welcome to use them to talk about Musa CAD, for example in reviews, articles, tutorials and courses.

Please don't use them in a way that suggests your own product, service or changed build is Musa CAD, or is endorsed by it. The licence lets you share a changed version, but give it a name of its own.

## Other names on this wiki

AutoCAD is a registered trademark of Autodesk, Inc. Musa CAD is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Autodesk.

This wiki mentions AutoCAD to help people who already know it, for example when a command works the same way. Everything here is written from Musa CAD itself, not from Autodesk's documentation.

Other names, such as ODA File Converter, LibreCAD, Qt, Flathub and GitHub, belong to their owners and are used only to name their products.
