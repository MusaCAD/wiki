## Maintainers

- **Pranay Kiran** ([@KiranPranay](https://github.com/KiranPranay)): author and maintainer; architecture, direction and review.

The full list of contributors is kept in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md) in the source repository. To be added, include your entry in your pull request. See [Contributing](/developer/contributing/).

## Built with

Musa CAD has its own geometry engine and depends on no other CAD library. It does stand on these open-source projects:

- **[Qt 6](https://www.qt.io/)** for the window, the ribbon and the dialogs.
- **Catch2** for its tests.
- **CMake** and **Ninja** to build it, and **linuxdeploy**, **flatpak-builder** and **NSIS** to package it.
- **[KDE's Flatpak runtime](https://flathub.org/)** for the Flathub version.

[Third-party components](/project/third-party-components/) lists every library with its licence.

This wiki is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), [Starlight](https://starlight.astro.build/) and [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app/).
