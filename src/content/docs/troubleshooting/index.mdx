## Before anything else

1. **Check your version.** Choose **About Musa CAD…** from the application menu (the Musa CAD logo at the top left), or run `musacad --version`. Many problems are already fixed in the [latest release](/releases/).
2. **Update.** Musa CAD checks for a new version once a day and shows it in the status bar. You can also choose **Check for Updates…** from the application menu. Flathub installs update with your other apps.
3. **Read the message.** Musa CAD says what went wrong instead of failing silently. The message usually names the cause, and these pages quote the messages so you can search for them.

## Still stuck?

If Musa CAD crashed, it offers to save a report the next time it starts. Attach it to a new issue. [How to report a bug](/troubleshooting/bug-reports/) explains what to include, and where the reports are kept.

You can also search the [existing issues](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues?q=is%3Aissue). Someone may have had the same problem.
