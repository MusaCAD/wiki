Open the Plot window with `PLOT` (or Ctrl+P), change one setting, and click **Preview…** to check before you plot again.

## The page is empty, or shows the wrong part

- **Plot area.** **Display** plots what is on screen now. **Extents** plots everything in the drawing. **Window** plots a rectangle you pick with **Pick Window &lt;**. Choose **Extents** if you are not sure.
- **Layers that do not plot.** A layer can be on screen but set not to plot. In the Layer Properties Manager (`LAYER`), check the **Plot** column. Layers that are off or frozen do not plot either.
- **A layout with nothing in its viewport.** When you plot a layout, Musa CAD plots the sheet at full size, with whatever its viewports show. Open the layout and check that the viewport shows your drawing.

## Every line has the same thickness

Tick **Plot object lineweights** in the Plot window. Then make sure your layers or objects have lineweights set. The **LWT** button on the status bar only changes the screen. It does not change the plot.

## Colours come out in colour, but should be black

Set **Plot style (CTB)** to **Monochrome (all black)**. **Grayscale** turns colours into shades of grey. From the command line, add `--monochrome` to `musacad --plot`.

## Dashed lines look solid, or the dashes are huge

The dashes are scaled by the linetype scale. If they are too small to see, they look solid.

- Type `LTSCALE` and try a bigger value, such as 10 for a drawing in millimetres. A smaller value makes the dashes shorter.
- In layouts, `PSLTSCALE` set to 1 scales dashes by each viewport's scale, so they look the same in every viewport.
- `MSLTSCALE` set to 1 makes dashes in model space follow the annotation scale.

## The drawing is the wrong size on paper

**Fit to paper** scales the plot area to fill the sheet. That is quick, but the drawing is not to scale. To plot at a real scale, untick **Fit to paper** and set **Scale**, for example 1 mm on paper to 5 drawing units for 1:5. Tick **Center the plot** to centre it on the sheet.

From the command line, `--fit` fills the sheet and `--scale 1:5` plots at a scale.
