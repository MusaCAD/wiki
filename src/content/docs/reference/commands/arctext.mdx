ARCTEXT places lettering along an existing arc. It creates separate TEXT characters and puts them in a group.

## How to use it

1. Draw an arc with [ARC](/reference/commands/arc/).
2. Type `ARCTEXT` and select the arc.
3. Enter the words and a positive text height.
4. Choose **Outward** or **Inward**.
5. Choose **Fit**, **Left**, **Right** or **Center**.
6. Enter the offset from the arc, or press Enter for zero.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Outward | Places the text on the outside of the arc. This is the default. |
| Inward | Places the text toward the inside of the arc. |
| Fit | Spreads the characters across the arc. |
| Left / Right / Center | Positions the lettering at its normal character spacing. |
| Offset | Distance from the arc to the text baseline. Must be zero or positive. |

For a simple example, place `MUSA CAD` on an arc with height `2.5`, Outward, Center and offset `1`.

## Good to know

The selected object must be an arc, not a circle or a polyline arc segment. An inward offset must leave a positive baseline radius.

The result is a group of ordinary text objects. It does not follow later edits to the source arc, and it is not one editable curved-text object. Undo removes the lettering as one operation. Use [TEXTEDIT](/reference/commands/textedit/) for individual characters, or recreate the lettering to change the whole phrase.
