ALIGNSPACE pans and scales a layout viewport so points in the model appear at chosen positions on the sheet. It changes the view, not the model geometry.

## Align one point

1. Switch to a layout tab and stay in paper space.
2. Type `ALIGNSPACE`.
3. Pick a point on the model as shown **inside a viewport**. This also identifies the viewport to change.
4. Press Enter at the second model-point prompt.
5. Pick where the first point should appear on the sheet.

The viewport pans to align that point and keeps its scale.

## Align two points

Give two model points, then their two corresponding paper-space points. The distance between the paper points sets the new viewport scale.

For example, if two model points are 100 units apart and their paper-space destinations are 50 units apart, the resulting scale is 1:2. Use matching directions for both pairs.

## Limits

- The first pick must be inside a viewport on the active layout.
- Version 0.9.0 adjusts **position and scale only**. It does not rotate the view to match the angle between the point pairs. If the angles differ, the command reports this.
- Use [U](/reference/commands/u/) to undo the viewport change.

See [Layouts and plotting](/user-manual/layouts-and-plotting/) for setting up the sheet and its viewports.
