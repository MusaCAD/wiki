U takes back the last change you made to the drawing. Run it again to go one more step back. If you undo too far, [REDO](/reference/commands/redo/) brings the step back.

## How it works

Type `U` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Musa CAD prints `Undo` and the drawing goes back one step. There is nothing to answer. Typing `UNDO` does exactly the same.

```musacad
Command: U
Undo
Command: U
Undo
Command: REDO
Redo
```

Here the two U's take back the last two changes, and REDO puts back the one the second U took away.

You can also undo with:

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>,
- the **Undo** button on the Quick Access Toolbar, the small row of buttons by the ribbon,
- **Undo** on the menu you get by right-clicking in the drawing.

Each of these prints `Undo` too. Redo has a **Redo** button next to **Undo**, and a **Redo** item on the same right-click menu. Its key follows your system: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> on Windows, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on Linux. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> does not redo on Linux yet; that is [issue #85](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/85).

## What one step is

One step is everything one command changed. If you drew four segments with one [LINE](/reference/commands/line/), one U removes all four. If you moved ten objects with one [MOVE](/reference/commands/move/), one U puts all ten back.

A few commands make each action its own step, so U goes back one action at a time. For example:

- each line of [TEXT](/reference/commands/text/),
- each click or drag in [TRIM](/reference/commands/trim/),
- each option you use in [PEDIT](/reference/commands/pedit/),
- each click or window in [MATCHPROP](/reference/commands/matchprop/),
- each edit in [TEXTEDIT](/reference/commands/textedit/).

## REDO

[REDO](/reference/commands/redo/) brings back the last step you undid. It also takes no answer and prints `Redo`. To bring back several steps at once, use [MREDO](/reference/commands/mredo/), which asks `Enter number of actions or [All/Last]:`.

Redo only works straight after undoing. As soon as you make a new change, the steps you undid can no longer be brought back.

## Good to know

- There is no fixed limit on how many steps you can undo. The history starts when you open or start the drawing.
- Each open drawing has its own history. Undo in one tab does not touch another tab.
- When nothing is left to undo, U still prints `Undo`, and nothing changes.
- U undoes changes to objects. Zooming and panning are not steps, so U does not change the view.
- Many commands have an **Undo** option of their own, such as LINE, TRIM and PEDIT. Typing `U` at one of their prompts uses that option, not this command.
- To bring back only the objects you last erased, use [OOPS](/reference/commands/oops/).

## Compared with AutoCAD

U works the way AutoCAD users expect: one step back each time. In Musa CAD, UNDO is another name for U. It takes back one step and asks nothing.
