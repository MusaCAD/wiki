CHAMFER cuts a corner off with a short straight line, called a bevel. You say how far back from the corner to cut on each line, and Musa CAD trims both lines and draws the bevel between them.

## How it works

Type `CHA` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Musa CAD shows the mode and the current distances. Set new distances with **Distance** if you need them. Then pick the first line and the second. Pick each one on the part you want to keep. The corner is bevelled and the command ends.

```musacad
Command: CHA
(TRIM mode) Current chamfer Dist1 = 0.0000, Dist2 = 0.0000
Select first line or [Undo/Polyline/Distance/Angle/Trim/mEthod/Multiple]: D
Specify first chamfer distance <0.0000>: 16
Specify second chamfer distance <16.0000>: 10
Select first line or [Undo/Polyline/Distance/Angle/Trim/mEthod/Multiple]: (pick the first line)
Select second line or shift-select to apply corner or [Distance/Angle/Method]: (pick the second line)
Chamfered.
```

The first distance is measured along the first line you pick, the second along the second line, both from the point where the lines meet or would meet.

While you move over the second line, Musa CAD shows the result a click would make. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> as you pick the second line to make a clean, sharp corner with no bevel this once.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Undo** (`U`) | Takes back the last chamfer made in this run. Useful with **Multiple**. |
| **Polyline** (`P`) | Bevels every corner of a 2D polyline at once. Asks `Select 2D polyline or [Distance/Angle/mEthod]:`, then reports how many corners it cut, such as `4 lines were chamfered.` |
| **Distance** (`D`) | Asks `Specify first chamfer distance <0.0000>:`, then asks for the second distance, offering the first one as its default. Switches to the Distance method. |
| **Angle** (`A`) | Asks `Specify chamfer length on the first line <0.0000>:`, then `Specify chamfer angle from the first line <0>:`. Switches to the Angle method. |
| **Trim** (`T`) | Asks `Enter Trim mode option [Trim/No trim] <Trim>:`. **Trim** cuts the lines back to the bevel. **No trim** leaves them as they are and only adds the bevel. |
| **mEthod** (`E`) | Asks `Enter trim method [Distance/Angle] <Distance>:` and switches between the two methods without changing any values. At the second-line prompt this option is **Method** (`M`). |
| **Multiple** (`M`) | Keeps asking for pairs, so you can bevel many corners. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to stop. Each corner is its own undo step. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> at **Select first line** | Ends the command. |

## Distance and Angle methods

CHAMFER can size the bevel in two ways.

- **Distance:** two distances, one along each line. Equal distances give a 45 degree bevel on a square corner.
- **Angle:** a length along the first line, and an angle measured from the first line.

The first line of each run shows the method in use. With the Angle method it reads, for example, `(TRIM mode) Current chamfer Length = 10.0000, Angle = 30`.

## Settings CHAMFER remembers

The distances, the length and angle, the method and the Trim mode are kept for the next time, for as long as Musa CAD stays open.

- The two distances are [CHAMFERA](/reference/system-variables/chamfera/) and [CHAMFERB](/reference/system-variables/chamferb/).
- The length and angle are [CHAMFERC](/reference/system-variables/chamferc/) and [CHAMFERD](/reference/system-variables/chamferd/).
- The Trim mode is [TRIMMODE](/reference/system-variables/trimmode/), which [FILLET](/reference/commands/fillet/) uses too. Changing it in one changes it in the other.

## Good to know

- CHAMFER works on two lines, on two segments of one polyline that meet, and on a whole polyline with **Polyline**. Anything else says `Chamfer: pick two lines, or two adjacent edges of one polyline.`
- Parallel lines are refused with `Chamfer: the two lines are parallel.`
- With both distances 0 and Trim on, the lines are trimmed or extended to meet in a sharp corner, the same as a <kbd>Shift</kbd> pick. With **No trim**, there is nothing to add: `Chamfer: nothing to add with zero distances in No trim mode.`
- On a polyline corner, distances longer than the segments are refused with `Chamfer: distances too large for that corner.` With **Polyline**, corners that are too short are skipped and counted at the end of the message.
- The trimmed lines keep their layer, colour and linetype. The bevel takes the lines' properties when both are on the same layer; otherwise it goes on the current layer.
- At the Trim mode prompt, <kbd>Enter</kbd> chooses **Trim**, even when the default shown is **No trim**. Type `N` to keep No trim.
- **Undo** takes back only the most recent chamfer. After that it says `Nothing to undo.`

## Compared with AutoCAD

CHAMFER uses AutoCAD's prompts, its Distance and Angle methods, and offers the first distance as the default for the second, as AutoCAD does. In Musa CAD it works on lines and polylines.
