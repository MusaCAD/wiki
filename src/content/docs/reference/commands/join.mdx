JOIN turns several objects into one. A chain of lines and arcs becomes a single polyline that moves, offsets and selects as one object. Two pieces of a broken line become one line again.

## How it works

The quickest way: select the objects first, then type `J` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Musa CAD joins them at once.

Or type `J` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> with nothing selected. Pick the source object, the one the result takes its layer and properties from. Then pick or window the objects to join to it, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

```musacad
Command: J
Select source object or multiple objects to join at once: (pick line 1)
Select objects to join: (pick lines 2, 3 and 4)
Select objects to join: ↵
4 objects joined into a polyline.
```

## What JOIN makes

What you get depends on what you join.

| You join | You get |
|---|---|
| Lines that all lie along one line | One line, from one far end to the other. Any gaps between them are filled. Musa CAD says, for example, `Joined 2 lines into one line.` |
| Arcs that all lie on one circle | One arc. The gaps between the arcs are filled, all but the largest one, which stays open. If they reach all the way round, a circle. |
| Elliptical arcs that all lie on one ellipse | One elliptical arc, filled in the same way, or a whole ellipse. |
| Splines of the same degree whose ends meet | One spline. |
| Lines, arcs and open polylines whose ends meet | One polyline. If the chain closes on itself, a closed polyline, and Musa CAD says `4 objects joined into a closed polyline.` |

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **cLose** (`L`) | Offered when the source is an arc or an elliptical arc: `Select arcs to join to source or [cLose]:`. Makes the source whole. Musa CAD says `Arc converted to a circle.` or `Elliptical arc converted to an ellipse.` |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> at **Select objects to join** | Joins what is selected and ends the command. |

## Good to know

- For a polyline, the ends must meet exactly. If none do, Musa CAD says `JOIN: the selected objects don't share endpoints.` Use object snaps when you draw, or close small gaps with [PEDIT](/reference/commands/pedit/) **Multiple**, **Join**, which asks for a fuzz distance.
- JOIN needs at least two objects it can join, or it says `JOIN: select at least two joinable objects (lines, arcs, open polylines).`
- Closed polylines have no free ends and are left as they are, as are objects JOIN cannot join, such as text.
- If the selection holds several separate chains, each becomes its own polyline, for example `Joined 6 objects into 2 polylines.`
- The new object takes the layer and properties of the source.
- The whole join is one undo step.

## Compared with AutoCAD

As in AutoCAD, lines on one line become one line, arcs on one circle become one arc, and a chain of mixed pieces becomes a polyline. JOIN itself has no fuzz distance; PEDIT's Multiple Join has one, as in AutoCAD.
