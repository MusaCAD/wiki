POLYGON draws a regular polygon: a shape whose sides are all the same length, such as a hexagon for a nut. The polygon is one closed polyline.

## How it works

Type `POL` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Give the number of sides, then the centre. Next, choose whether the size you give reaches the corners (**Inscribed**) or the middle of the sides (**Circumscribed**). Then give that size.

```musacad
Command: POL
Enter number of sides <4>: 6
Specify center of polygon or [Edge]: 0,0
Enter an option [Inscribed in circle/Circumscribed about circle] <I>: ↵
Specify radius of circle: 20
Polygon created.
```

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edge** (`E`) | Instead of the centre, give the two ends of one side: `Specify first endpoint of edge:` and `Specify second endpoint of edge:`. Looking from the first point to the second, the polygon is built on your left. Give the points from left to right to build it above the edge. |
| **Inscribed in circle** (`I`) | The radius is the distance from the centre to each corner. The polygon fits inside a circle of that radius. |
| **Circumscribed about circle** (`C`) | The radius is the distance from the centre to the middle of each side. The polygon fits around a circle of that radius. For a hexagon 20 across its flats, type a radius of 10. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> at **Enter number of sides** | Keeps the number shown, which is the last one you used. |

## Typing or clicking the radius

- If you type the radius, the polygon stands on a flat bottom edge.
- If you click a point, the point sets both the size and the turn. With **Inscribed**, a corner lands on the point. With **Circumscribed**, the middle of a side does.

## Good to know

- A polygon can have 3 to 1024 sides. Any other number is refused with `Requires an integer between 3 and 1024.`
- The number of sides and your Inscribed or Circumscribed choice are kept until you close Musa CAD. Pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> at the Inscribed or Circumscribed question keeps your last choice, but the prompt always shows `<I>`. Type `I` or `C` when you want to be sure.
- A radius of 0 or less is refused with `The radius must be greater than zero.` Two edge ends at the same place are refused with `The two edge endpoints must differ.`
- The result is a closed polyline. To work on its sides as separate lines, use [EXPLODE](/reference/commands/explode/).
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the command. Nothing is drawn.

## Compared with AutoCAD

POLYGON follows AutoCAD's two ways of sizing a polygon: about a centre, Inscribed or Circumscribed, or by one edge. As in AutoCAD, a typed radius stands it on a flat bottom edge.
