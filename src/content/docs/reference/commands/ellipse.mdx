ELLIPSE draws an ellipse, the oval shape a circle makes when you look at it at an angle. It can also draw part of one, called an elliptical arc. An ellipse has two axes: the long one through its widest part, and the short one across it.

## How it works

Type `EL` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Give both ends of one axis. Then give the distance from the centre out to the edge along the other axis. Musa CAD draws the ellipse and the command ends.

```musacad
Command: EL
Specify axis endpoint of ellipse or [Arc/Center]: 0,0
Specify other endpoint of axis: @80,0
Specify distance to other axis or [Rotation]: 20
```

The distance is half the other axis. This ellipse is 80 long and 40 tall.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Center** (`C`) | Give the centre first: `Specify center of ellipse:`. Then `Specify endpoint of axis:`, one end of the first axis, and the distance to the other axis. |
| **Arc** (`A`) | Draws an elliptical arc. You give the ellipse in the same way, then a start and an end angle. See [Elliptical arcs](#elliptical-arcs). |
| **Rotation** (`R`) | Instead of the other axis: `Specify rotation around major axis:`. Think of a circle as wide as the first axis, turned away from you by that angle. 0 gives a circle, and 60 makes the other axis half as long. The angle must be less than 89.4 degrees. |
| **Isocircle** (`I`) | Appears only while isometric drafting is on (see [ISODRAFT](/reference/commands/isodraft/)). Draws a circle as it looks in the current isometric plane. Give the centre, then the radius or **Diameter**. |

## Elliptical arcs

Type `A` first. After the axes, Musa CAD asks for the start and end angles:

```musacad
Command: EL
Specify axis endpoint of ellipse or [Arc/Center]: A
Specify axis endpoint of elliptical arc or [Center]: C
Specify center of elliptical arc: 150,0
Specify endpoint of axis: @40,0
Specify distance to other axis or [Rotation]: 20
Specify start angle or [Parameter]: 0
Specify end angle or [Parameter/Included angle]: 270
```

Angles are in degrees, measured counter-clockwise from the first axis you gave. The arc runs counter-clockwise from the start angle to the end angle. You can also click a point: its direction from the centre gives the angle.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Parameter** (`P`) | Give the ellipse's parameter instead of a true angle. The parameter is the angle on the circle the ellipse is squashed from, which differs from the true angle except along the axes. |
| **Angle** (`A`) | Goes back to true angles. |
| **Included angle** (`I`) | At the end prompt, give how far the arc sweeps from its start instead: `Specify included angle for arc <180>:`. |

## Good to know

- The longer axis always becomes the major axis, whichever one you gave first. Angles for an arc are still measured from the first axis you gave.
- An axis with no length is refused with `Invalid axis length.`
- **Rotation** of 89.4 degrees or more is refused with `Rotation must be less than 89.4 degrees.`
- **Isocircle** while isometric drafting is off says `Isocircle needs isometric drafting: ISODRAFT, or SNAP Style Isometric.`
- An included angle of 0 is refused with `Included angle must be non-zero.`
- When [PELLIPSE](/reference/system-variables/pellipse/) is 1, ELLIPSE draws a polyline made of arcs that follows the ellipse, instead of a true ellipse.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the command. Nothing is drawn.

## Compared with AutoCAD

ELLIPSE follows AutoCAD's prompts. As in AutoCAD, the longer axis is stored as the major one, **Rotation** accepts angles below 89.4 degrees, and **Isocircle** is offered while isometric drafting is on.
