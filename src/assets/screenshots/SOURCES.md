# Screenshot sources

These pictures come from the engine repository, `assets/screenshots/`, at commit 742295f
("assets: the screenshots taken again for v0.8.0", 2026-10-23). They are made by the
application itself (`tools/screenshots/capture.sh`, `MUSACAD_LISTING_SHOT`) from drawings
written by `tools/screenshots/make_drawings.py`, so they carry no third-party drawing.

Licence: LGPL-3.0-or-later, as the engine repository's assets are. They are used here under
that licence and are not relicensed by the wiki's CC BY-SA 4.0.

Captured with: Musa CAD 0.8.0. Pages that use them set `capturedWith: 0.8.0` on the component.

|---|---|
| overview.png | The window: ribbon, three drawing tabs, a house plan with a wall selected, the Properties palette |
| command-entry.png | `REC` typed at the cursor with RECTANG and RECTANGLE offered |
