# Screenshot sources

The original 0.8.0 pictures come from the engine repository, `assets/screenshots/`, at commit 742295f
("assets: the screenshots taken again for v0.8.0", 2026-10-23). They are made by the
application itself (`tools/screenshots/capture.sh`, `MUSACAD_LISTING_SHOT`) from drawings
written by `tools/screenshots/make_drawings.py`, so they carry no third-party drawing.

Licence: LGPL-3.0-or-later, as the engine repository's assets are. They are used here under
that licence, which is also the wiki's licence.

Captured with: Musa CAD 0.8.0. Pages that use them set `capturedWith: 0.8.0` on the component.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| overview.png | The window: ribbon, three drawing tabs, a house plan with a wall selected, the Properties palette |
| command-entry.png | `REC` typed at the cursor with RECTANG and RECTANGLE offered |
| dynamic-input.png | A wide polyline drawn with its length and angle at the cursor, polar tracking at 45° |
| architectural-plan.png | A house plan with its south elevation and room schedule |
| mechanical-detail.png | A dimensioned flange with section A-A, a bolt circle and geometric tolerances |
| hatch-section.png | The Hatch Editor tab over a wall and strip-footing detail |

## Musa CAD 0.9.0

`dynamic-input-0.9.0.png` is copied from `assets/screenshots/dynamic-input.png` at
[the v0.9.0 tag](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/blob/v0.9.0/assets/screenshots/dynamic-input.png).
It shows the named Length and Angle fields, blue active value, polar tracking and
keyboard hints. It uses the same project-owned sample floor plan and LGPL-3.0-or-later
licence. Pages using this image set `capturedWith="0.9.0"`.
